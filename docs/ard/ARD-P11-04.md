# ARD-P11-04: IAS 12 uncertain tax treatments and tax-rate reconciliation

## Status

Implemented within a bounded calculation and disclosure-provider boundary.
Independent professional tax and accounting review remains pending; this is
not a claim of full IAS 12 or IFRIC 23 compliance.

## Scope and decisions

- The host supplies the unit-of-account decision (individual or group), its
  rationale, the probable-acceptance assessment, and evidence references. A
  grouped unit is represented by one treatment with aggregate scenarios.
- The model requires acknowledgement that the tax authority is assumed to
  examine the relevant amounts with full knowledge of all relevant information.
  The engine does not make legal, tax-law, or probability judgments.
- When acceptance is assessed as probable, the recognized tax position remains
  the amount reported in the tax return; no amount is adjusted merely because
  an acceptance probability exceeds a threshold.
- When acceptance is not probable, the host selects and justifies one method:
  the most likely amount selects the unique highest-probability outcome; the
  expected value computes the exact probability-weighted amount. Probabilities
  must be within `[0, 1]` and sum exactly to one. Ambiguous most-likely results
  fail instead of choosing arbitrarily.
- Tax position amounts are signed: positive amounts are liabilities and
  negative amounts are tax assets. The output separates increases and decreases
  for each balance, avoiding treating a reduction in a payable as an invented
  receivable.
- Reassessment is a new immutable measurement. Changed assessment inputs or an
  expired limitation date trigger reassessment; prior and updated measurements
  and the reason are returned together for host-side audit retention.
- `Reconcile` now returns period and line-level permanent-difference effects.
  Callers can attach the measured uncertain tax treatments to the reconciliation
  so the structured IAS 12 disclosure includes their reported/recognized
  positions and adjustment, alongside statutory and effective rates,
  current/deferred expense, permanent differences, and an explicitly labelled
  unallocated reconciling difference. The host-provided current and deferred
  tax expense totals must already reflect recognized effects; attached
  uncertainty measurements are disclosure details and are not added a second
  time. The IAS 12 module exposes the
  `DisclosureProvider` interface for configured, company/period-scoped
  reconciliation records.
- The module calculates and formats host-supplied data only. It does not
  determine local tax law, prepare/file returns, persist assessments, post
  journals, or replace a qualified tax adviser.

## Golden scenarios and evidence

| Scenario | Expected result | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Probable acceptance | Reported tax amount retained unchanged, with zero adjustment | `TestMeasureUncertainTaxTreatmentProbableAcceptanceUsesFiledAmount` |
| Binary uncertainty | Most likely outcome selected and liability adjustment calculated | `TestMeasureUncertainTaxTreatmentMostLikelyAmountBinaryScenario` |
| Multiple possible outcomes | Expected value equals the exact probability-weighted tax amount | `TestMeasureUncertainTaxTreatmentExpectedValueMultipleOutcomes` |
| Liability reduction and tax-asset increase | Liability decrease is distinguished from a separately measured increase in a reported tax asset | `TestMeasureUncertainTaxTreatmentReducesReportedLiabilityWithoutInventingAsset`, `TestMeasureUncertainTaxTreatmentCalculatesTaxAssetIncrease` |
| Reassessment | Changed facts and limitation dates trigger a new measurement while retaining the prior result | `TestUncertainTaxTreatmentReassessmentOnFactsAndLimitation` |
| Invalid assumptions and scenario data | Missing full-knowledge assumption, incomplete probabilities, tied maximum, or mixed currencies are rejected | `TestUncertainTaxTreatmentRejectsMissingEvidenceAndInvalidOutcomes` |
| Tax-rate reconciliation | Structured disclosure includes line-level differences, uncertainty adjustments, tax effects, rate availability, and a reconciling residual | `TestBuildTaxReconciliationDisclosure` |
| Provider scope and registry | Provider filters company and reporting period; registry validates and hashes the result | `TestTaxReconciliationDisclosureProviderScopesAndRegistry` |
| Module integration | IAS 12 module exposes the disclosure through `DisclosureProvider` | `TestModuleProvidesTaxReconciliationDisclosure` |
