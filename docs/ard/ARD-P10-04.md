# ARD-P10-04: Foreign currency transactions and monetary remeasurement

## Status

Implemented within the valuation package's calculation and draft-entry boundary.
This is not a claim of full IAS 21 compliance.

## Scope and decisions

- Initial conversion uses the typed `SPOT` rate through `ConversionEngine`.
- Period-end remeasurement is limited to positions explicitly classified
  `MONETARY`. `NON_MONETARY` positions are skipped by the batch operation;
  missing and unknown classifications fail rather than defaulting to monetary.
- Remeasurement compares the closing-rate value with the current functional
  carrying amount. Callers must feed forward the carrying amount after each
  posted remeasurement.
- Settlement entries debit/credit the monetary account at its current carrying
  amount. `RealizedFXSettlementMove.CarryingAmount` supports prior period-end
  remeasurement; when omitted, the original recognition rate supplies the
  carrying basis. For partial settlements, the caller supplies the carrying
  value attributable to the settled foreign amount.
- The signed settlement difference is cash settlement value minus carrying
  value. The account nature (receivable/payable) determines whether a positive
  or negative movement is a gain or loss.
- Closing-rate results preserve `ExchangeRate` provenance and quote inversion
  when the provider supports `GetRateRecord`; the generated draft's
  `DocumentReference` includes quote, type, effective date, applied rate, and
  source when available. A provider implementing only `RateProviderSPI` does
  not expose the original source record, so only the request and returned rate
  can be referenced.
- Settlement moves can carry original-recognition and settlement rate records
  (including effective dates and sources) and inversion flags; the generated
  draft records both. If the caller supplies only numeric rates, the draft
  records those values but cannot claim unavailable source/date provenance.
- Generated revaluation and settlement entries are validated drafts. This
  package has no persistence or posting dependency; the host must submit drafts
  through its approved application/posting flow.

Foreign operation translation, group consolidation, FCTR, and automatic
creation/posting of invoice entries are out of scope. See
[`ias21_scope_clarification.md`](../compliance/ias21_scope_clarification.md).

## Golden scenarios and evidence

| Scenario | Expected calculation and entry | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial USD invoice | USD 10,000 × spot 3.75 = SAR 37,500 | `TestIAS21_MonetaryPayableLifecycleRevaluationAndSettlement` |
| Payable period-end remeasurement | Carrying SAR 37,500; close 3.80 gives SAR 38,000, SAR 500 unrealized loss; debit loss and credit AP | Same test |
| Payable settlement after remeasurement | Carrying SAR 38,000; settle 3.82 gives SAR 38,200, SAR 200 additional realized loss; debit AP SAR 38,000, credit bank SAR 38,200, debit loss SAR 200 | Same test |
| Receivable settlement gain | EUR 20,000 at 4.10 to 4.25 yields SAR 3,000 gain | `TestIAS21_MonetaryReceivableSettlementRealizedGain` |
| Payable settlement gain | Carrying SAR 380; pay SAR 370; credit gain SAR 10 | `TestIAS21_PayableSettlementRealizesGainWhenCarryingAmountExceedsCashPaid` |
| Receivable settlement loss | AR SAR 375; collect SAR 370; debit loss SAR 5 | `TestIAS21_ReceivableSettlementRealizesLoss` |
| Non-monetary position | Historical-cost non-monetary item produces no closing-rate result; direct calculation is rejected | `TestIAS21_NonMonetaryItemsNotRevaluedAtClosingRate` |
| Historical rates | As-of queries select the latest eligible typed rate, retain source/effective date, and identify an inverse quote | `TestIAS21_RateRegistryHistoricalReproducibilityAndProvenance`; revaluation output provenance is asserted in the lifecycle test |

Generated journal entries in the realized and unrealized scenarios pass
`JournalEntry.Validate`, which checks line validity and functional-currency
balance. The tests verify the intended accounts and debit/credit values. They
do not persist or post those drafts.
