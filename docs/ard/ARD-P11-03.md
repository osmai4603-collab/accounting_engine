# ARD-P11-03: IFRS 16 lessor accounting, lessee exemptions, and disclosure payloads

## Status

Implemented for the bounded scope declared in this ARD. The P8/P9/P10
prerequisites (`ModulePorts`, atomic posting, year-end close, disclosure
framework) are complete and accepted. Posting integration for standard modules
is owned by P11-05 and remains explicitly unimplemented.

## Decision

Extend the IFRS 16 module (`internal/ifrs16`, `modules/ifrs16`) from a
lessee-only scope to three additional bounded capabilities, following the
architectural pattern agreed for IFRS 9 in P11-02: judgments stay with the host
system and arrive as approved, final inputs; the engine applies the resulting
accounting deterministically.

1. **Lessor accounting (host-classified).** The engine does not assess the
   transfer of risks and rewards. The host supplies the classification
   (`operating` or `finance`) as a final input and the engine applies the
   corresponding measurement:

   - **Operating lease** (`OperatingLeaseContract`): lease income is
     recognized straight-line over the lease term regardless of the cash
     pattern, with a signed contra balance — an accrued rent receivable when
     income runs ahead of cash and a deferred lease income liability when cash
     runs ahead of income. The leased asset stays on the lessor balance sheet
     and is depreciated straight-line over the host's depreciation policy;
     initial direct costs incurred in obtaining the lease join the depreciated
     asset base and are expensed on the same basis as the lease income. A
     depreciation horizon longer than the lease term intentionally leaves a
     carrying amount at lease end.
   - **Finance lease** (`FinanceLeaseContract`): the leased asset is
     derecognized at commencement in favor of the net investment in the lease —
     the present value of receipts plus the present value of the unguaranteed
     residual value plus initial direct costs, discounted at the implicit rate.
     The difference between the fair-value equivalent (net investment minus
     initial direct costs) and the asset carrying amount is booked as a
     derecognition gain or loss. Finance income is allocated at the implicit
     rate; the final period absorbs the rounding residual and the amortization
     of initial direct costs so the closing balance equals the unguaranteed
     residual value exactly. Realizing the returned residual after lease end
     remains a host disposal operation.

2. **Lessee exemptions.** Short-term (at most twelve months, no purchase
   option, payments inside the term) and low-value (asset fair value at or
   below a host-configurable threshold) exemptions are modeled as an
   alternative accounting treatment: payments are charged to profit or loss
   straight-line, with the expense-versus-cash difference accruing as a lease
   expense liability or a prepaid lease asset. The exemption election itself is
   a host accounting-policy choice; the engine validates mechanical eligibility
   only.

3. **Disclosure payloads.** Deterministic builders produce
   `reporting.StructuredDisclosure` payloads for the P9-05 framework: the lease
   expense and income note (host-supplied balances) and the future lease
   payments maturity analysis derived from the lessee schedule
   (`FutureLeasePaymentBuckets`, `LeaseCarryingLiability`). The disclosure
   registry test proves the payloads pass normalization, scope matching, and
   content-hash generation.

## Draft generation and idempotency

`LessorEntryBuilder` and `ExemptionEntryBuilder` mirror the existing
`EntryBuilder` contract: they require a host-backed posting capability and a
resolved configured journal mapping before creating drafts, resolve accounts
through the capability, and produce deterministic entry identities via
`identifyEntry` with distinct operation tags (`lessor-operating-idc`,
`lessor-operating-income`, `lessor-operating-depreciation`,
`lessor-finance-commencement`, `lessor-finance-receipt`,
`exemption-expense`). Rebuilding a draft for the same lease reference and
operation reproduces the same ID, idempotency key, and payload hash; durable
retry enforcement remains the host application's transactional responsibility.
No builder posts or persists entries, and no workflow claims
`ProducesJournalEntries`.

## Compliance matrix updates

`docs/accounting/ifrs16_compliance_matrix.md` documents the lessor models, the
exemptions, the disclosure payloads, and the explicit exclusions: sale and
leaseback and subleases, lessor classification assessment, residual
realization, index/rate reassessments, impairment, and full transition. The
module descriptor moves `IFRS16-LESSOR-ACCOUNTING` out of the unimplemented
list into three bounded supported requirements (`IFRS16-LESSOR-OPERATING-01`,
`IFRS16-LESSOR-FINANCE-01`, plus the exemption and disclosure requirements),
declares `IFRS16-SALE-LEASEBACK` and `IFRS16-DISCLOSURE-PROVIDER` as
unimplemented, and keeps `IFRS16-POSTING-WORKFLOW` unimplemented for P11-05.
The module version is bumped to 1.1.0 (SemVer minor: backward-compatible
additions).

## Consequences and boundaries

- Module-level `DisclosureProvider` retrieval requires durable company lease
  state, which `ModulePorts` does not provide; the module therefore does not
  implement the provider interface in this task. Payload construction is
  delivered and tested, and retrieval is completed with the P11-05 posting
  workflow.
- Finance income allocation uses the implicit rate on the net-investment
  balance; a zero-rate contract with initial direct costs cannot amortize them
  through income and is rejected by the schedule instead of silently producing
  a negative income period.
- Straight-lining produces equal income per period with contra balances rather
  than re-allocating cash; cumulative contra balances always clear to zero at
  lease end.
- Independent professional accounting review of the expanded scope has not yet
  been received; the descriptor and this ARD state that limitation.
